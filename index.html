A small experiment, separated from the official board

Three VoxConverse v0.3 test recordings were scored with two self-hosted pyannote pipelines. This view contains real pilot results, but it is not an official leaderboard ranking: the sample is tiny and public audio may have appeared in training data.

Community-1 aggregate DER
9.87%

vs 13.87% for pyannote 3.1

Relative DER improvement
28.9%

4.00 percentage-point absolute reduction

What the pilot suggests

Community-1 won on two of three recordings and improved aggregate DER mainly through lower speaker confusion: 4.19% vs 8.20%. False alarm and missed speech were nearly unchanged. It lost on aepyx, so this is directional evidence—not a universal winner claim.

Methodology at a glance

The official board is designed around representative, unseen data and transparent evidence.

Primary metric domain-balanced DER
Reference two annotators + adjudication
Uncertainty paired session bootstrap
Hard speech overlap and short turns retained
Tracks offline, live, multichannel
Maintenance versioned protocols
